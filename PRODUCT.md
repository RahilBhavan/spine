# Spine

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform and stack
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, served from the repository root. Python builds the public data snapshots. Plotly is the only external production script; no frontend build step.

## Purpose and evidence
Spine investigates the nine Morpho Blue markets on Base that fund loans in Coinbase's app. It combines current book observations with modeled liquidation capacity and saved-book historical crash replays. It is independent analysis, not affiliated with Coinbase or Morpho.

## Users and jobs
The user selected risk monitoring and market investigation as the primary audience: identify where capacity is tight, inspect changes, compare markets, and understand the assumptions behind stress results. No operator interviews or adoption are claimed. Borrowers can also check a Base address; researchers can inspect the writeup and public sources.

## Constraints
Preserve all existing data, chart/table alternatives, loan checks, supplier exposure, market coverage, source dates and limitations. Do not present estimates as observations, a hypothetical backstop as a commitment, historical replay as a forecast, or underwater exposure as end-of-path loss. Never infer causality from snapshot differences. Existing blue identity and light/dark support remain authoritative. No wallet connection, keys, backend account, pricing, invented customer claims, or new data provider.

## Success
A reader can find the selected market's capacity threshold and modeled loss, compare it with the other markets, and locate the relevant assumptions. Keyboard users can complete the same investigation. The phone supports the full workflow. Human comprehension remains a separately recorded test.
